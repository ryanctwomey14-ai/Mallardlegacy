Accredited investors only

Offerings are made under Rule 506(c) of Regulation D. Participation is limited to persons who qualify as accredited investors under Rule 501, and accreditation must be verified by a third party before subscription is accepted.

Risk of loss

Private real estate investment involves substantial risk, including the following, which is not an exhaustive list:

  • You may lose some or all of your invested capital.
  • Interests are illiquid. There is no public market, no redemption right, and transfers require sponsor consent.
  • Investments use leverage, which magnifies both gains and losses.
  • Distributions are not guaranteed and may be reduced or suspended at any time.
  • Returns depend on the sponsor's judgement, on local employment and housing conditions, on interest rates, and on insurance and operating costs outside our control.
  • Tax treatment depends on your individual circumstances and may change with legislation.

Targets are objectives, not guarantees, projections or promises of performance. Actual results will differ, potentially materially.
